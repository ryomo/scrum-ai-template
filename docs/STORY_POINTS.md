# Story Point Examples

Past PBIs to compare against when estimating.
Size by the scope touched, how much needs checking, and uncertainty, not by lines of diff
(a small diff in a core part can be bigger than a large mechanical one).

If an item looks like 13 points or more, consider splitting it into sub-issues.

## 1pt

One cause or one place to change, little to verify.

## 2pt

A fix within one mechanism, with some investigation or a few paths to verify.

## 3pt

Adds one new mechanism or UI component, but the scope is closed.

## 5pt

Uncertainty that needs investigation along the way, or several new things to build.

## 8pt

Touches the core of the product, or spreads across the whole codebase.
